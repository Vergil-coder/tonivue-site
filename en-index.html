Set your volume

Select an audio output, enable local audio processing, and adjust the in-app volume slider. System audio is processed on your Mac.

Dim your display

Software dimming adjusts the appearance of external displays. Compatible brightness keys can control the external display under your pointer.

A voluntary 14-day free trial is planned, followed by a one-time purchase to permanently unlock the current features listed at purchase.

The trial requires an explicit purchase to continue paid adjustments after expiry. Mute and restore controls remain available. Final pricing and supported devices will be published at launch.
