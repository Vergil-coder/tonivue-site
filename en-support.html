How do I adjust volume?

Select an output, start your trial or restore a purchase, then enable local audio processing. Allow system audio recording when macOS asks. Use the volume slider in the app.

The slider moves but volume stays the same.

Check that processing is enabled, the system default output matches your selected physical output, and the player uses the system default output. Check audio recording permission in System Settings under Privacy & Security. Temporarily quit other utilities that automatically change your output. The current engine requires matching sample rates and Float32 stereo audio. Other formats may not start.

Can I use the macOS volume slider?

The Store edition uses its own volume slider. macOS may still show HDMI or DisplayPort volume as unavailable. Open Tonivue to adjust it.

How do I restore direct output?

Pause playback or lower the volume in your player first. Select the option to restore direct system output and confirm. The device or player returns to its original volume, which may be louder.

How does display brightness work?

Software dimming changes the appearance of an external display. Hardware backlight remains controlled by the display itself. Use Restore Brightness to restore the image.

How do brightness keys work?

Enable compatible brightness keys in the app and move your pointer to the target external display. The Fn combination depends on your keyboard mode and macOS function-key settings. If a key is not recognized, use the in-app slider and email support with your keyboard model.

Will the trial charge me automatically?

You start the trial explicitly. It lasts 14 consecutive days. After expiry, a one-time purchase is required for new paid adjustments. Restoring the trial with the same Apple Account preserves the original start date.

How do I restore a purchase or request a refund?

Open the trial and lifetime purchase window and choose Restore Purchases using your original Apple Account. Apple handles refund requests at Report a Problem.
