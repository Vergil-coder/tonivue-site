Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · Tonivue Store edition 1.0

Scope

This policy covers the Tonivue Store edition and this support website, provided by Vergil Lu. The app adjusts compatible audio volume and external display dimming on your Mac.

Audio and device information

When enabled, volume processing receives system audio and applies gain locally on your Mac. Audio is used for real-time processing, is not saved as recordings, and is not sent to developer servers. Device identifiers, volume, and display state help identify devices, show controls, and restore output.

Local records

The app stores the most recently observed time locally to check trial-time consistency. The app may write diagnostic device state, volume, audio peaks, and frame counts to the app sandbox. These records contain no audio samples. The app has no feature to upload these records to the developer.

System permissions

Volume processing requires macOS system audio recording permission. Display dimming is handled locally. Compatible brightness keys use system keyboard interfaces. You can manage audio recording permission in System Settings.

Trials and purchases

Apple processes Mac App Store purchases. The app reads verified StoreKit product details, original purchase dates, and refund or revocation state to determine trial and lifetime access. Apple handles payment details under its own policies.

Support requests

When you email vergilluw@gmail.com, your address, message, and any attachments you choose to send pass through email services to help resolve your request. Share only what is needed for troubleshooting. Avoid passwords and complete payment details.

Website visits

This static website provides product, support, and privacy information. It includes no developer-operated analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses, requested paths, and access times to deliver pages, protect security, and diagnose errors, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Email links open your chosen email app.

Your data and policy changes

You can delete local app settings and diagnostic files, or email support to request deletion of support information you have sent to the developer, subject to legal retention requirements. Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date.

Contact

Developer Vergil Lu. Send privacy questions to vergilluw@gmail.com.
